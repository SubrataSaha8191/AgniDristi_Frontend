# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AgniDrishti serves NTRO analysts monitoring persistent or abnormal thermal sources, CPCB and SPCB officers checking industrial compliance, NDMA teams assessing regional risk, and SIH evaluators reviewing the system. Users need to move quickly from a regional overview to a defensible source-level assessment while working in a dense geospatial intelligence dashboard.

## Product Purpose

AgniDrishti turns FastAPI outputs from FIRMS monitoring, VNF processing, thermal fingerprinting, and classification pipelines into an operator-facing geospatial dashboard. Success means users can distinguish live monitoring data from training evidence, isolate high-priority gas-flare candidates, inspect the supporting evidence without overstating certainty, and understand pipeline health from one coherent interface.

## Brand Personality

Operational, rigorous, and direct. The interface should feel like a credible command-center tool: high-signal, decisive, and technical without becoming theatrical or obscure.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative glass surfaces, neon sci-fi control panels, soft consumer-app styling, and any presentation that implies certainty unsupported by the backend. Avoid hiding important states behind animation or relying on color alone.

## Design Principles

1. Put operational decisions first: surface status, severity, uncertainty, and the next useful action.
2. Show evidence with its provenance: clearly distinguish FIRMS monitoring, VNF processing, GEM context, and heuristic assessment.
3. Preserve spatial context: filters, map markers, lists, and object details should behave as one synchronized workspace.
4. Make uncertainty legible: nulls, likelihoods, confidence, and missing evidence must remain explicit.
5. Design for incident-speed scanning: dense information should remain readable, keyboard-operable, and responsive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible focus states, keyboard operation, accessible labels, reduced-motion preferences, and text or shape cues in addition to color. Preserve readable contrast in both supported themes and ensure responsive layouts remain usable on narrow screens.
